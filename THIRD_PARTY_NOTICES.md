# Third-Party Notices

This repository does not bundle the upstream Skill implementations listed in `registry/skills.json`.

Names, descriptions, repository links, and license identifiers are provided for curation and installation. Copyright in each upstream project remains with its authors and contributors.

Installable entries currently point to projects under permissive licenses including MIT and Apache-2.0. The installer resolves upstream source at installation time and copies discoverable upstream license/notice files into the installed Skill directory.

Reference-only projects are not automatically cloned when their license is unverified or when they are intentionally kept as design references.

Third-party datasets, figures, logos, icons, journal names, conference names, and trademarks may have terms distinct from the repository-level software license. Always inspect the upstream project's own notices before reuse or publication.
